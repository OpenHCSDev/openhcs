# Figure assembly

The current figure order, captions and included assets are owned by
[the manuscript](manuscript.md) and
[the supplementary material](supplementary/README.md). Use those documents,
not the earlier three-figure proposal, when assembling or revising the paper.
The current manuscript has eight main figures, including four on fresh-context
analysis. Autonomous results belong in the main text, not only in the supplement.

## Main-text story

Keep the shared-workflow and editable-analysis figures as the platform
introduction. The neurite example and throughput figure then demonstrate
operation and scale. The subsequent figures supply the scientific evidence:

- **Task-only segmentation:** show matched raw/first/final native views beside
  the scored repair and complete 200-field distribution. Keep difficult and
  annotation-empty fields in the distribution. The original three-field repair
  and final whole-corpus coverage answer different questions.
- **Local repair:** show the repaired defect with a genuine-neighbour control
  and a remaining failure. A successful repair should be visible without relying
  on the caption's interpretation.
- **Retinal analysis:** retain useful localisation in heterogeneous background
  and the caught pair-merging regression. Do not turn uncertain boundaries into
  a blanket rejection of supported detections, or present localisation as a
  manually validated census.
- **Translocation:** show the recovered assay response with well-level
  variability. Distinguish this fresh-context repeat from the separately frozen
  prospective held-out experiment.

Keep numerical results in their existing score receipts, plot data and captions;
do not maintain another score table in this plan. The next assembly pass should
make independent-repeat variability easy to find alongside within-run gains.
Choose panels for their evidence and legibility, not a fixed figure-count target.

## Supplementary detail

Use the supplement for distributed raw/result/combined witnesses, volumetric
XY/XZ/YZ review, full paired-field repeat comparisons, exact pipeline and capture
identities, and development continuations. The stitched personal-neurite mosaic
is useful development evidence; its assisted history remains explicit and does
not become a fresh autonomous stitching result by moving the panel.

Selected CellProfiler output parity, biological agreement and throughput remain
separate measurements. Use the current manuscript's evidence definitions rather
than the old plan's proposed speedup or module-coverage claims.

## Editing and delivery

Follow the sibling papers repository's
`docs/papers/writing_style_guide.md`: lead with checked results, use plain
language, and place necessary qualifications at the claim they constrain.
Reuse each existing figure generator and provenance receipt. Never retouch a
native screenshot to improve apparent segmentation; retain its documented crop,
source, presentation and candidate identity.

After changing a figure, inspect the bitmap and its actual PDF page at readable
scale. Check panel labels, text collisions, raw/result alignment and caption
agreement. Use the existing paired manuscript/supplement builder for changed
reading copies; source-only planning changes do not require a scientific rerun
or an unnecessary document rebuild.

instructions when preparing submission.
