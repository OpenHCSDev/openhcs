## Method and measurement definitions

Paired raw uint16 DAPI(w1) and FITC(w2) planes enter the registered neurite_outgrowth_metaxpress operation. Named source bindings join well/site/Z/time identities. FITC is the neuronal and body channel; DAPI supplies nuclear support. Declared XY spacing is 1.3556 µm/pixel, preserved in artifacts and native viewer transforms; it was not independently recalibrated. No external preprocessing or display rescaling enters detection. The operation embeds background responses, enhancement, adaptive thresholding, body/nuclear admission and owned rooted tracing.

Body controls: approximate maximum width 35 µm, minimum area 40 µm², local background intensity excess 1000, minimum inscribed diameter 5 pixels. Nuclear controls: approximate width 4–25 µm, local background excess 1000. Outgrowth controls: maximum width 4 µm, local background excess 100, enhanced candidate threshold correction 0.025, no separate hysteresis seed correction. These widths control native operations; they are not a manual geometric truth label. The complete source and pinned contract hashes govern other native defaults. Two workers, streaming disabled, lossless DEFLATE level 1 storage.

A detected cell is an admitted nuclear-supported FITC soma label. All such rows are retained, including zero-process cells and boundary cells; no manual count or independent neuronal phenotype is asserted. A process is one soma-adjacent rooted partition including admitted daughter arms, not each graph edge. Arbor length sums owned paths outside soma interiors in physical units. A branch event is a qualified junction with three distinct image corridors including the proximal soma arm, clustered at shaft-width scale. Native qualification excludes crossing cores, shared corridors and foreground-disc skeleton stars. Same-plane fluorescence cannot establish every biological overlap or owner; graph colours are operational assignments.

Per-cell exports retain processes, total arbor length, mean/median/maximum process length, qualified branches, body area, source identities and other native fields. Per-field exports retain detector channels, all counts, trace/ownership diagnostics and original raw paths/hashes. Per-well exports give field means/medians, pooled cell-weighted summaries and explicitly marked observation sums. Absent-process length sentinels are excluded from eligible-cell process statistics; zero total length is retained. Missing fields are never replaced by zero. Field-edge truncation shortens recoverable paths, while nuisance spurs and ownership errors can bias measurements in either direction.
