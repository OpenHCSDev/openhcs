Description
-----------

Supporting material for the OpenHCS manuscript: frozen agent-authored analysis
pipelines and post-freeze evaluations, retained microscopy presentation panels,
reproducible figure-generation scripts, and matched CellProfiler comparison and
timing records. Independent task-only trials and assisted demonstrations are
identified separately. Reference annotations and treatment responses have
different scopes, documented in their evaluation records. The archive preserves
the final selected benchmark record and the single generated manuscript claim
include; historical measurements are labelled separately.

Contents and exclusions
-----------------------

tables, frozen scientific source and reproducibility manifests needed by the
supplement; retain directory relationships so local archive links resolve.

environment directories, model-session transcripts containing private paths

panels must retain their source credit and declared processing. Preserve
third-party licences; OpenHCS's licence does not relicense the underlying
datasets. Check the assembled archive for missing referenced files before
uploading, not just for successful compression.
