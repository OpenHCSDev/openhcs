# Neurite comparison — email paragraph

The smaller outgrowth fold changes do not indicate a systematically weaker recovery of the drug response. At 40 µM FC-A, MetaXpress measured a 74.6 µm increase in outgrowth per cell, compared with 80.1, 71.7 and 101.6 µm in analyses 1–3. With Y27632, the increases were 77.2 µm for MetaXpress and 83.0, 101.4 and 108.6 µm for the analyses. The agent analyses started from higher vehicle values: across the four vehicle wells, they measured 21,935–32,291 µm of outgrowth per site, compared with 13,682 µm for MetaXpress. Analyses 1 and 3 had similar mean cell counts (186 and 180 against 188); analysis 2 detected fewer cells (153). Pooling cells across the nine sites instead of averaging the site means changed the fold changes by at most about 0.06 (maximum 0.0618). Branching is a different result: from zero dose to 40 µM, analyses 1–3 added 1.46, 1.26 and 2.44 branches per cell with FC-A, and 1.57, 2.42 and 2.65 with Y27632, against MetaXpress's 2.94 and 2.90. Analysis 3 came closest; analyses 1 and 2 also recovered smaller absolute branching increases. These comparisons do not establish which method traces more accurately. Manual tracing and the MetaXpress analysis settings are needed to explain the baseline differences; the saved agent detections, settings and source images remain available for inspection.
